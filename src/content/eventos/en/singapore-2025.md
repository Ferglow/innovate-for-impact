## A concert with purpose

Singapore was IFI's first international expansion outside the Americas, bringing the conversation on conscious business to the heart of Southeast Asia.

The edition combined panels on sustainable innovation with a benefit concert that transformed the stage ideas into a collective, unforgettable experience.

### Highlights

- Asia-Pacific leaders in conscious business
- Benefit concert closing the evening
- Strategic partnerships with regional organisations

A night that proved sustainability and creativity move forward better together.
