## Where it all began

The first edition of Innovate for Impact was born during Climate Week in New York in 2024, with a clear ambition: to prove that innovation can drive a sustainable future.

Attendees from companies like Apple, Google, Amazon and UNICEF participated in panels, exclusive dinners, and collaborative activities that laid the foundations of the IFI community.

### Highlights

- Inaugural panel on conscious business and profitability
- Tree-planting initiative for every attendee
- Birth of the global changemakers community

Each attendee planted a tree: the impact started before the event ended.
