## Where business, nature & innovation converge

The fourth edition of Innovate for Impact returned to New York during Climate Week, cementing itself as the meeting point between business leaders and the global sustainability agenda.

Over two days, 25 speakers took the stage across 5 panels exploring how to integrate sustainability into the core of business models—from corporate strategies to community projects.

### Highlights

- Panels with leaders who have embedded sustainability into their core strategy
- An immersive soundbath experience to close the sessions
- Networking spaces with attendees from Apple, Google, Deloitte and more

The New York edition reaffirmed IFI's purpose: inspiring real action where innovation meets impact.
